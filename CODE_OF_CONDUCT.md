# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
